# DT2 Boss indicator
This plugin notifies players of perfect kill failures.

## Vardorvis utilities
- Hide the arena pillars, with tile markers where they stood
- Hide any of the 8 axe spawns: static axes, moving axes, or both, per direction
- Mute the sounds of hidden axes
- Highlight axes as hull, tile, true tile, or outline, with separate border and fill colors; axes you hide stay unmarked
- Highlight each head by attack style: mage (default purple) and ranged (default green)
- Spore captcha feedback: the spore under your cursor lights up, plus an outline on all spores if you want it
- Swap the Whisperer's and Vardorvis' head prayer-disable projectiles to any of ~24 boss styles (Inferno, CoX, ToB, ToA, Leviathan, Doom of Mokhaiotl, Hueycoatl, Hunllef, and more)
Made using the wiki's guidelines for perfect kills:

## Leviathan
- Any damage besides roar after sequence of attacks

## Whisperer
- Any non-zero damage

## Duke Sucellus
- Being frozen by extremities/shadow magic
- Duke magic attacks
- Duke gaze attack
- Melee slam (not chip damage)
- Vents

## Vardorvis
- Prayer disabling projectiles
- Axe hits
- Spike hits
- Captcha failure
- Attacks off prayer
